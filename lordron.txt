The rain may fall and blur the road,
The winds may carry a heavy load,
Yet I will walk through every storm,
Holding on to the person I become.

For every scar, a lesson stays,
For every night, there comes a day.
And though the world may shift and bend,
I will keep writing until the end.

Not every battle needs a sound,
Some victories are quietly found.
So with each word and every line,
I leave a piece of this heart of mine.
— Lord Ron
