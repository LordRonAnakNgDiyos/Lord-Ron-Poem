When The Sun Learns To Leave

The sun was leaving slowly,
painting the sky with colors
I knew would not stay.

I stood beneath the fading light,
watching shadows grow longer
as if even the day
was learning how to let go.

There are goodbyes
that never need to be spoken.

Sometimes, people simply become memories
while they are still standing beside you.
And maybe that is why
I have always loved the sunset—

it reminds me that endings
can still be beautiful,
even when
they leave the sky
a little darker than before.
