The Things I Never Said

There are words I keep beneath my tongue,
sentences I write but never send,
feelings that remain unfinished
because I am afraid of where they might end.

I have mastered the art of smiling
when my thoughts are falling apart,
of saying “I’m okay”
while carrying storms inside my heart.

Maybe some things are meant to stay unspoken,
not because they mean nothing,
but because they mean too much
to be placed into ordinary words.

So I write instead—
hoping every line can carry
what my voice could never explain,
and every empty space can hold
the things I was too afraid to say.

Perhaps one day,
I will no longer need silence
to understand myself.

Until then,
I will keep writing.
